## Background

More or less the same as [P](https://p.elianiva.com), but with support to render Pi export.

It's written in Astro, deployed in CF Workers, and uses Turso to store the pasted text.

I was initially thinking of using `astro:db` but it didn't work out so I swapped it with Turso.
