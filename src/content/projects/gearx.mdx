## Background

A web-based dashboard for CarX Street — featuring suspension and gearbox tuning calculators, with more tools coming soon. Based on StepUnique's calculator.

Most of it was vibe coded by Opus. I wanted a simple calculator to help me calculate all the tuning when I'm playing CarX Street. May or may not be accurate, but it's helpful as a starting point.
